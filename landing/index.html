FAQ

Questions, answered

What exactly do I get?

The complete source code of OpenHive (TypeScript, Hono, D1), database migrations, docs for deploying to Cloudflare, the REST API and MCP server, and future updates for the version you bought.

Is this a subscription?

No. You pay once and own your copy. Your only running cost is your Cloudflare usage — many newsletters fit in the free tier, and Workers Paid starts at $5/month.

Do I need to know how to code?

You need to be comfortable running a few terminal commands. The deploy guide walks through every step: create the database, enable email sending for your domain, deploy.

How does email sending work?

OpenHive sends through Cloudflare Email Sending on your own domain. Cloudflare publishes SPF and DKIM for you; the deliverability guide covers DMARC, warmup and feedback loops.

Do I need Stripe?

Only to take real payments. Without Stripe keys everything runs in test mode so you can try paid tiers, products and tickets end to end. With Stripe Connect, creators get paid directly.

Can I run newsletters for clients?

Yes. One instance hosts unlimited workspaces and publications, each with its own domain, team and roles. You can even set your own platform fee.

Can I move my existing list?

Yes — import subscribers from CSV (with custom fields and tags), and podcasts from any RSS feed with episode GUIDs preserved.

Is my data mine?

Completely. It lives in your Cloudflare account. Export subscribers, segments and posts at any time.
